# NNNN. A short, specific title naming the actual decision

## Status

Accepted (YYYY-MM-DD). (Or: superseded by `decisions/NNNN`, once that
record exists — added as a note here, the rest of this file's content
left untouched.)

## Context

What situation made a decision necessary? What were the real
constraints — technical, time, prior commitments? Enough detail that a
reader with no memory of this moment can understand why *some* choice
had to be made here.

## Decision

What did you actually decide, stated plainly? If there's a specific
mechanism (a chosen data structure, a chosen library, a chosen
process), name it concretely rather than describing it abstractly.

## Alternatives considered

What else did you seriously consider, and why did you rule each one
out? This is often the most useful section later — the "why not the
obvious thing" answer.

## Consequences

What does this decision commit you to? What becomes harder, what
becomes easier, what would need to change if this decision were ever
reversed?
